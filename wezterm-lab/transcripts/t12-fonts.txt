$ wezterm ls-fonts --codepoints 2764    # a heavy black heart
LeftToRight
 0 ❤    \u{2764}     x_adv=10 cells=1  glyph=uni2764,2883 wezterm.font("DejaVu Sans Mono", {weight="Book", stretch="Normal", style="Normal"})
                                      /usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSansMono.ttf, FontConfig

Reading it: the heart is NOT in the built-in JetBrains Mono, so
WezTerm fell back to DejaVu Sans Mono, found on the system by
FontConfig. That fallback chain is what ls-fonts exists to expose.
